Save Your Memories

When you download your data from social media, your photos and videos are often trapped in files that are hard to manage. This tool scans the files and finds your memories, converting photos from clunky HTML links into ready-to-use JPEGs and preserving your videos all in one place - and without them ever leaving your computer. Simple, private, and in your hands.

Why We’re Here

Hi! I’m a new mum, and like many parents, having a baby changed how I see the world - especially the digital one.

I’ve always known that social media platforms use our data, but reading about how they target children while holding my little one made me realize I couldn't turn a blind eye anymore. I wanted a way to protect our family's private memories without having to disappear from the internet entirely.

It’s not about leaving. It’s about a more intentional relationship with our data.

I’m not "anti-social media." I love using these platforms to support local businesses and stay inspired. But there is a massive difference between media (news, art, business) and memories (our children and private lives).

Take back your data

I created this project to give you an easy way to:

Retrieve your history

Quickly recover your photos, videos, and memories directly in your browser.

Clear the slate

Privacy Policy

Your trust is our priority. Here is how we protect your information:

  • Local Processing: All photo extraction happens entirely in your browser. Your images and personal data are never uploaded to our servers.
  • No Tracking: We do not use cookies or tracking scripts to monitor your activity.
  • External Links: Donations are handled by secure third-party platforms. Please review their policies when donating.

Since your data never leaves your computer, you remain in total control of your memories at all times.
